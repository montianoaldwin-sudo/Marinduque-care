About MARINDUQUE CARE

MARINDUQUE CARE is a health and wellness educational website designed for residents, students, and families in Marinduque. It promotes awareness of everyday healthy habits and general wellness information.

Healthy Living Tips

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Maintain a regular sleep routine.
  • Practice proper personal hygiene.
  • Choose balanced meals when available.
  • Include suitable physical activity in your routine.

Health Resources

For personal health concerns, consult a qualified health professional or an appropriate local health facility.
