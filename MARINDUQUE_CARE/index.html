Everyday wellness tips

Simple reminders for supporting everyday well-being.

◉

Hydration

Keep drinking water available and drink regularly. Individual needs can vary with activity, weather, and health circumstances.

✿

Balanced meals

When possible, enjoy a variety of foods, including vegetables, fruits, and other locally available nourishing foods.

☾

Rest and routine

A consistent daily routine can make it easier to make time for sleep, relaxation, and activities you enjoy.
